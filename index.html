The idea, in plain words

Schools and companies keep years of courses, learners, grades and certificates in an old LMS. Moving them is like moving house with valuables: one dropped box can become a compliance problem. Lyncs is the careful moving company.

Every record ends in one of five places. Nothing is lost silently.

The Lyncs architecture on one page

Data moves left to right through five zones. The old system is only ever read. Gates stop the work from moving on until something has been checked.
